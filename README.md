Rocketbench

Rocketbench is a browser rocket simulator for people who like designing and flying rockets.

You stack parts to design a rocket, set staging, and launch it into a time step flight simulation over a round planet with a thermal model and parachutes. There is a free flight mode and missions such as First Hop, Stratosphere, Soft Return, Karman Line, Bullseye, Reentry, Round Trip, and High Ground.

Latest change. 2026-11-27, v2026.11.27. Version 2 added a round planet with orbits, a thermal model, drogue and main parachutes, and rocket tiers with a hangar. The orbit map now shows live known satellite orbits (ISS, Tiangong, Hubble, GPS, geostationary) with a nearest satellite readout. Added Match the ISS and Hubble Shell missions, and a Pajamastan Space Administration link with a live space weather GO or HOLD badge.
